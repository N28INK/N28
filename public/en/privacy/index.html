Who we are

City Cars Houston TX connects Houston car buyers with licensed Texas dealers. We are not a dealer or a lender. Our only official WhatsApp is (281) 602-7044.

What we receive

If you use "Build your plan" and send the message: your first name, your city, the vehicle you're interested in, your situation (optional), ranges for down payment, income and time at your job, and which types of documents you have (optional). We never ask for Social Security, ITIN, driver's license or passport numbers, or bank account details.

How it reaches us

This site doesn't store your answers on any server and doesn't use tracking cookies. Your plan is put together on your own device and only reaches us if you send it on WhatsApp, a Meta service with its own privacy policy. Vehicles you save with the heart stay in your browser only.

How we use it

To reply on WhatsApp or by phone and connect you with licensed dealers who work with your situation.

Who we share it with

Only with partner dealers, and only with your authorization, either through the "I authorize sharing this information with partner dealers" checkbox or in writing in the chat. The type of ID you have can reveal sensitive information, so that question is optional and we don't share it without your permission. We don't sell your information.
